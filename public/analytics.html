Current Season Power Rankings

Unlike standard standings which only look at total wins, this Power Ranking calculates who is playing the best right now. It weights Season Win % (40%), Point Differential Percentile (30%), and Last 5 Form Win % (30%) to generate a definitive 0-100 rating.

Current Season Expected Wins (Luck vs Skill)

A formula originally created by Bill James for baseball, adapted for RDA. By ignoring actual game outcomes and purely analyzing a team's Total Points Scored vs Total Points Allowed, the math predicts how many games a team should have won. We subtract Expected Wins from Actual Wins to calculate how "Lucky" or "Unlucky" a franchise has been this season.

Current Season Bench Efficiency

Measures a GM's ability to set the optimal lineup. We calculate the mathematical highest possible score a team could have achieved each day (by summing their top 8 highest-scoring players across all active sports) and compare it against the 8 starters the GM actually played. The closer to 100%, the better the GM is at playing the right matchups.
